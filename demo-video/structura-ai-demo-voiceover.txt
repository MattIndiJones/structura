Voice-over script (English) — TP Advisory Services

NOTE ON THIS FILE
-----------------
No voice-over was recorded for the delivered MP4. No professional text-to-speech
engine was available in the build environment, and a poor synthetic voice would
have cost more credibility than silence gains. The video is therefore designed
to be fully self-sufficient without sound: every message appears on screen.

This script is provided so a human voice can be recorded later and laid over
"structura-ai-demo-no-voice.mp4" without re-editing the picture. Timings match
the delivered cut.

Reading pace: unhurried, low-key, institutional. Roughly 145 words per minute.
Pause on each full stop. Do not "sell" — state.


[00:00 - 00:10]  OPENING
Structured products start as a financial idea.
Turning that idea into a working quantitative implementation is where the time
goes.

[00:10 - 00:26]  ARCHITECTURE
STRUCTURA shortens that step.
Its AI-assisted layer translates a product description, or a term sheet, into an
executable product script — built from predefined quantitative building blocks.
The AI does not price anything. It writes the definition.

[00:26 - 00:47]  PRODUCT INPUT
Here, a five-year worst-of autocall on three indices.
The structurer states the product in plain financial terms — maturity,
observation frequency, coupon, autocall level, protection barrier.
The model runs locally: the description never leaves the desk.

[00:47 - 01:10]  AI TO SCRIPT
The generated script is the product definition, in full.
Observation schedule, coupon, autocall condition, barrier, redemption logic —
all explicit, all editable.
A control sheet states what the script actually does, so a plausible script that
describes the wrong product cannot pass unnoticed.
Change a parameter, and it is the structurer's change, not the model's.

[01:10 - 01:24]  PRICING
From here, the quantitative engine takes over.
Monte Carlo pricing, with a confidence interval — on twenty thousand paths, in
about two seconds.

[01:24 - 01:40]  RISK AND SCENARIOS
Sensitivities, payoff profile, simulated paths.
Understanding the product beyond a single price.

[01:40 - 01:52]  LIFECYCLE
The same definition carries through to the observation schedule and to lifecycle
monitoring.
One product definition. One consistent workflow.

[01:52 - 02:03]  CLOSE
STRUCTURA.
Describe. Generate. Price. Monitor.
AI-assisted implementation, built on a controlled quantitative architecture.
By TP Advisory Services.


WORDS TO AVOID — non-negotiable
-------------------------------
Never say, in any variation:
  "the AI prices the product"
  "AI-powered pricing"
  "the model calculates the value"
The AI writes the product definition. The engine computes the price. The whole
positioning rests on that distinction; blurring it in the narration undoes what
the picture is showing.
